The feed is infinite.
Your time is not.

We have traded boredom—the birthplace of creativity—for cheap dopamine. We scroll past our lives, five seconds at a time, lost in a curated ecosystem of bright lights and variable rewards.

It is not a lack of willpower; it is a battle against engineering. You are fighting a supercomputer designed to keep you in a state of passive consumption.

Consider this your exit sign.

We are the friction in the loop. This project is a digital deep breath designed to snap you out of the trance. We are building tools not to capture your time, but to defend it.

Please Don't Scroll.

Every feed you use runs on a variable reward loop, the same mechanism as a slot machine.

That's not a metaphor, it's the design brief. Teams of engineers are paid to keep you scrolling past the point you meant to stop, and willpower alone doesn't hold up against systems built by people whose only job is to beat it. What works instead is structure: boundaries you don't get to renegotiate in the moment you're most likely to give in.

That's the whole premise here.

Every scroll you don't take back is an hour you don't get back either: hours that were going toward work that matters, people in your life, or just sleep. Please Don't Scroll treats that time like the asset it is, and gives you two ways to protect it: friction for everyday discipline, and a stricter mode for when discipline needs to be enforced, not negotiated.

Decide once. Let the system hold the line.

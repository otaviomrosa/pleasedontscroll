Questions

Good to know

What happens when a session ends?

Everything opens again. Nothing is blocked outside a session, so there is nothing to pause or switch off.

Can I end a session early?

A Friction session, yes, with two clicks. A Strict session runs until the timer ends, and so does a scheduled Strict session.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from Settings in your dashboard. Pro stays on until the end of the billing period, then your account moves to Free.

What happens to my profiles if I go back to Free?

They are kept and locked. Your first profile keeps working, and the rest come back if you subscribe again.

Do you see what I browse?

No. The extension checks each site against your list on your own computer. We only store the list, your settings and your account.

Frequently asked

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your billing portal at any time. You keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period, then your account moves to the Free tier. No penalties, no questions asked.

Is my data private? Do you sell it?

Never. Your blocklist and activity data stay in your personal account. We do not sell, share, or analyze your data for advertising purposes. The whole point of Please Don’t Scroll is to protect your attention, and that principle extends to how we handle your information.

What happens to my profiles if I downgrade to Free?

Your profiles are preserved. The extension will fall back to your first profile. If you re-subscribe later, all your custom profiles are exactly where you left them.

Does the extension work without a Pro subscription?

Absolutely. The core blocking and breathing friction experience is free forever. Focus Pro adds convenience and depth: multiple profiles, Strict Mode, and upcoming analytics. The fundamental tool for reclaiming your attention costs nothing.
